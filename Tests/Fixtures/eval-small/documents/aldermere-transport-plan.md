# Aldermere Transport Plan 2025–2035

## Metro Line 3

Metro Line 3 will connect the Harbour district with the university campus in the north over 22 kilometres and 14 stations. Tunnelling begins in March 2026 and the line is expected to open in 2029. The project has a budget of 4.1 billion euros, of which the national government will pay 55 percent. At opening, planners forecast 190,000 trips per day and a journey time of 31 minutes from end to end.

## Buses and fares

The council will introduce a flat fare cap of 2.80 euros per day for buses, trams and metro combined, replacing the zone system that has been in place since 1998. A new night bus network begins in 2026; route N7 will run every 30 minutes between the central station and the airport from midnight to 5 a.m. Electric buses will make up the whole fleet by 2032, replacing 410 diesel vehicles.

## Cycling and walking

The plan adds 120 kilometres of protected bike lanes by 2030, bringing the network to 310 kilometres. The Old Bridge will close to cars in 2027 and reopen as a pedestrian and cycling route. Bike parking for 8,000 bicycles will be built at the 20 busiest stations, and a public bike-share scheme with 3,000 bikes launches in 2027.

## Congestion charge

From January 2028, drivers entering the inner ring road will pay 5 euros per day on weekdays between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. Residents of the zone receive a 90 percent discount. Revenue is reserved by law for public transport and is expected to reach 38 million euros in the first year. Electric vehicles are exempt until 2031.

## Consultation

More than 11,000 residents responded to the public consultation in 2024. The most requested change was a station at the Eastfield retail park, which has been added as a possible extension to Line 3 if funding allows.
